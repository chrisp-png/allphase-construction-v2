## Start With an Honest Answer: Do You Need a New Roof Yet?

Some homeowners who call us about affordability are surprised to hear that their roof doesn't need replacing yet. A targeted repair can sometimes keep a roof watertight while you plan and save, so you aren't paying for a replacement before you need one.

Other times, the roof really is done. Many Fort Lauderdale homes still have roofs that went on decades ago. When a roof has failed in several places, paying for repair after repair can cost more over time than replacing it.

Before you look at grants or financing, find out which situation you're in. A free, no-obligation roof assessment from All Phase Construction USA will tell you:

- Whether the roof can be repaired or needs to be replaced
- What a repair would cost now, and what replacement would cost
- Which problems are urgent and which can wait
- What condition the decking, flashing and roof-to-wall connections are in

We tell you what the roof needs, not what we'd like to sell. Sometimes the honest answer is "repair it and budget for replacement later."

## Watch Out for "Free Senior Roof Replacement" Ads

This fall we've had more calls from homeowners, many of them retirees, asking about a "free roof" or a "senior roof replacement grant" they saw in a social media ad.

Be careful with these ads. A real government program has published eligibility rules, an official application, and a named agency that runs it. If an ad promises a free roof to anyone over a certain age, asks for personal information before telling you who runs the program, or pushes you to sign quickly, stop and check it before you go any further.

- Look for the program on an official state, county or city website (.gov)
- Call the agency directly using a phone number you looked up yourself
- Never sign a contract or hand over documents to "lock in" a grant
- Ask any contractor which program they're talking about and how it's funded

If you aren't sure whether something is real, call us. We'll tell you what we know, even if it doesn't lead to a job for us.

## Option 1: My Safe Florida Home

All Phase Construction USA was one of the original My Safe Florida Home contractors, and we still do program work today. The program, run by the Florida Department of Financial Services, helps homeowners strengthen their homes against hurricanes. It starts with a free home inspection, and qualifying homeowners may then be eligible for grant money toward the improvements that inspection recommends.

What to know, based on the program's published rules as of November 2026:

- **The grant is capped at $10,000.** For most homeowners it's a matching grant: the state pays $2 for every $1 you pay. Low-income homeowners can qualify for up to $10,000 with no match.
- **Eligible roof work** is roof deck attachment, a secondary water barrier and roof-to-wall connections. If the roof covering has to come off to do that work, the program counts replacing the whole roof section as part of the project. Patching and partial repairs are never eligible.
- **Who qualifies:** a site-built single-family home or townhouse that's your homesteaded primary residence, built before January 1, 2008, with an insured value of $700,000 or less.
- **Applications open in phases,** starting with low-income homeowners 60 and older. The program reports it is accepting applications, but funding and phases change, so check the current status at [mysafeflhome.com](https://mysafeflhome.com).
- **Don't start work before you have written grant approval.** Work begun before approval isn't reimbursed.

On our side, MSFH jobs start with a site visit and attic assessment, followed by a line-item proposal with a milestone payment schedule. Because we hold both a roofing license and a general contractor license, we can do the hurricane strapping and structural hardening work that roofing-only companies can't. Our senior discount can be combined with a My Safe Florida Home project.

## Option 2: City and County Home Repair Programs

Several local governments in Broward and Palm Beach Counties run housing rehabilitation or emergency repair programs for income-qualified homeowners, usually funded through the state SHIP program or federal housing dollars. Some of them cover roofs. Funding opens and closes in rounds, so call before you count on one.

- **City of Fort Lauderdale:** the city's Housing Rehabilitation program has listed roof replacement among eligible repairs, as a deferred, interest-free loan for income-qualified homeowners. Applications are taken online. Housing office: (954) 828-6024.
- **Broward County:** the county's Minor Home Repair program for income-qualified homeowners includes roof repair and replacement. Its most recent pre-application window ran through September 2026. Housing and Urban Planning Division: (954) 357-4900.
- **Palm Beach County:** the Department of Housing and Economic Development runs owner-occupied rehabilitation and SHIP emergency repair rounds. Whether roof work is covered depends on the round. HED: (561) 233-3600.
- **Your own city:** many cities, including North Lauderdale, Weston, Margate and Plantation, run their own programs.

These programs usually have income limits, waitlists, and their own contractor selection process. Apply early, and keep copies of everything you submit.

We covered one local example in our [North Lauderdale roof grant post](/blog/north-lauderdale-roof-grant).

## Option 3: Federal Programs for Seniors and Low-Income Homeowners

The USDA Section 504 Home Repair program offers loans of up to $40,000, and grants of up to $10,000 for homeowners 62 and older, to repair or improve their homes. It's for very low-income households, generally below 50% of the area median income. Eligibility also depends on whether your property is in an area the USDA classifies as rural, so most of urban Broward and coastal Palm Beach County won't qualify. Parts of western Palm Beach County may. Your local USDA Rural Development office can tell you whether your address is eligible.

## Option 5: PACE Financing (In Some Cases)

PACE (Property Assessed Clean Energy) financing is a less common option that occasionally fits. Instead of a regular loan, the cost of a qualifying improvement is repaid through a special assessment added to your property tax bill. Florida law lists roof repair and replacement, including roof hardening work like deck attachment, secondary water barriers and roof-to-wall connections, as qualifying improvements for homes. In our area, PACE providers include Ygrene and Home Run Financing.

PACE is a loan, not a grant, and it comes with real tradeoffs. Under Florida law (section 163.081, Florida Statutes):

- **Your city or county has to have authorized PACE,** and each provider has its own rules. Ygrene, for example, says you need at least 10% equity in your home.
- **The total PACE assessment can't exceed 20% of your home's just value** as set by the property appraiser, unless your mortgage lender agrees in writing to more.
- **The yearly payment can't be more than 10% of your household income,** and income has to be verified.
- **You must be current on your mortgage and property taxes,** with no involuntary liens on the property.
- **PACE isn't available if the home has a reverse mortgage.** Many seniors do, so check this first.
- **The repayment term can't exceed 20 years,** and there are no balloon payments or prepayment penalties.
- **The assessment becomes a lien on the property,** collected with your property taxes. If you sell or refinance, the lender may require you to pay it off. If it goes unpaid, it is treated like unpaid property taxes, which can ultimately put the home at risk.
- **If you plan to leave the home to family,** any remaining balance stays with the property.
- **You have 3 business days to cancel** after signing, and the provider must go over the terms with you on a recorded phone call before approval.

Because the payment rides on your tax bill, compare the total cost with regular financing before you choose PACE, and never let anyone rush you into signing. We'll walk you through both if PACE looks like a fit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there really a free roof replacement program for seniors in Florida?**
We aren't aware of a program that gives every senior a free roof. Some programs offer grants or low-interest loans to homeowners who meet age and income requirements. Check any "free roof" ad against an official .gov source before you share personal information.

**Does All Phase offer a senior discount?**
Yes. Homeowners 65 and older automatically receive $750 off any project of $25,000 or more. See our [senior roof discount page](/senior-roof-discount).

**Do I have to ask for the senior discount?**
No. It's applied automatically for homeowners 65 and older. Mentioning it when you call helps us make sure it shows on your proposal. We confirm age from the photo ID you already show us at signing for the notarized permit application.

**Can I combine the senior discount with financing or My Safe Florida Home?**
Yes, with both.

**Does My Safe Florida Home pay for a new roof?**
Not by itself. It pays for recommended hardening work: deck attachment, a secondary water barrier and roof-to-wall connections. When the roof covering has to come off to do that work, replacing the whole roof section can count toward the grant. Every application starts with the program's free home inspection.

**How do I know if I need a new roof or just a repair?**
A professional assessment. Age is one factor, but the condition of the underlayment, decking, flashing and fasteners matters more. We offer free, no-obligation assessments.

**Can I finance a roof replacement?**
Yes. All Phase offers flexible financing options for qualified homeowners. Use our [financing calculator](/easy-payments) to estimate payments.

**What if I can't qualify for financing?**
Ask about adding a co-signer, such as a family member who plans to inherit the home. You can also look at local housing repair programs, PACE financing in some cases, and whether a repair can safely buy you time while you apply.

**What is PACE financing, and can it pay for a roof?**
PACE lets you repay a qualifying improvement, including a roof replacement, through an assessment on your property tax bill. In Florida, the total can't exceed 20% of your home's just value, the yearly payment can't exceed 10% of your household income, and it isn't available with a reverse mortgage. It places a lien on the property, so compare it carefully with regular financing.

**Is it smart to keep repairing an old roof?**
For a few small problems, often yes. When problems are widespread, repeated repairs can add up past the cost of replacement. An honest assessment will tell you which applies.

**How much does a roof replacement cost in Fort Lauderdale?**
It depends on the roof type, size, pitch and the condition of the decking. Our [roof cost calculator](/roof-cost-calculator) gives a city-specific range, and an assessment gives you an exact price.

**Do you serve all of Broward and Palm Beach Counties?**
Yes. We're based in Deerfield Beach and dual-licensed to work throughout both counties.

**What should I do if a contractor pressures me to sign today to "lock in" a grant?**
Don't sign. Real programs have published rules and timelines, and none of them depend on a same-visit signature.
