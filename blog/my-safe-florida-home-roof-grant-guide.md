# Is There a Free Roof Replacement Grant in Florida? What My Safe Florida Home Actually Covers

**There is no state program that hands Florida homeowners a free new roof. There is a real one, My Safe Florida Home, that can pay a large share of specific hurricane-hardening work, and much of that work happens during a roof replacement. Here is how it works, who qualifies, and how a roof project runs in Broward and Palm Beach County.**

## The Short Answer

If you searched "free roof replacement grant," you have probably already landed on several pages that never answered the question. Here it is in plain terms:

- **My Safe Florida Home (MSFH)** is a state program run by the Florida Department of Financial Services. It funds hurricane mitigation improvements on eligible homes, subject to money the Legislature appropriates each year.
- **The grant is capped at $10,000.**
- **For most applicants it is a matching grant, not free money.** The state reimburses $2 for every $1 the homeowner pays, up to the $10,000 cap. Low-income homeowners can qualify for a grant of up to $10,000 with no match required.
- **It pays only for improvements recommended in your program inspection,** and the program lists exactly four. The roof covering itself, the tile, shingles or metal, is not one of them.
- **Applications open in phases,** starting with the highest-priority groups. Check current status at [mysafeflhome.com](https://mysafeflhome.com) before you plan around a grant.

## Who Qualifies for My Safe Florida Home

There are two steps to qualify for: the free inspection, and then the grant. These are the program's published rules for the current funding year.

| Requirement | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Home type | A site-built single-family home on its own parcel, or a townhouse. Condos, mobile homes, duplexes and rentals do not qualify. |
| Occupancy | Your primary residence, with a Florida homestead exemption |
| Age of home | Built before January 1, 2008 (required for the grant) |
| Insured value | $700,000 or less (required for the grant) |
| Income | Grants are prioritized for low-income homeowners (at or below 80% of county median income) and moderate-income homeowners (below 120%), with homeowners 60 and older first in each group |
| Inspection | You must complete the program's free hurricane mitigation inspection before applying for a grant |

If your home was built in 2008 or later, it was built under a newer edition of the Florida Building Code and does not qualify for the grant. The free inspection has no income test, so a homeowner who isn't eligible for a grant can still get one.

## What the Grant Can Pay For on a Roof

The program funds four improvements, and only the ones your initial inspection report recommends. Three of them are roof work:

- **Roof deck attachment:** re-nailing the roof deck so it resists uplift in high wind.
- **Secondary water resistance:** a self-adhered underlayment sealed to the deck, which limits water intrusion if the roof covering is damaged.
- **Roof-to-wall attachment:** clips or straps that tie the roof structure to the walls.

The fourth is **opening protection**: impact-rated windows, doors, garage doors and skylights. That work is done by window and door contractors, but it counts toward the same $10,000 grant.

Here is why a roof replacement and an MSFH grant so often go together. Once the old roof comes off, the deck is exposed, and deck nailing, the secondary water barrier and roof-to-wall connections are all reachable at the same time. The grant can help pay for those parts of the job. The new roof covering itself is still the homeowner's cost, and the program is explicit that no other construction work is funded.

## How the Process Works, Step by Step

The program has a set order, and doing things out of order can cost you the grant. The most important rule: **do not start any work before you receive written grant approval.** Work begun before approval is disqualified from reimbursement.

1. **Create an account** on the program portal at mysafeflhome.com and complete the short prioritization questionnaire.
2. **Wait to be placed in an inspection group,** then complete the inspection application.
3. **Have your free hurricane mitigation inspection.** The program assigns a licensed inspector; you cannot use your own.
4. **Review your inspection report.** It lists which improvements are recommended for your home.
5. **Apply for the grant,** including your contractor's name and state license number and your current homeowners insurance declarations page.
6. **Wait for written approval.** Only then should work begin.
7. **Have the approved work completed** by a licensed contractor. You have 18 months from approval to finish the work and request the final inspection.
8. **Request the final inspection** through the portal. You get one opportunity, so make sure all work is complete and permits are closed first.
9. **Submit your draw request** with the contractor's invoice and your insurer's discount information. Matching-grant homeowners also submit proof of payment in full; low-income grants do not require it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a free roof replacement grant in Florida?**
Not in the sense most people mean. My Safe Florida Home is the main state program. For most applicants it is a matching grant capped at $10,000, and it pays for specific hurricane-hardening items, not the roof covering. Low-income homeowners can qualify for up to $10,000 with no match.

**How much does My Safe Florida Home pay?**
Up to $10,000. For matching grants, the state pays $2 for every $1 the homeowner pays, until it reaches $10,000.

**Will the grant pay for my whole new roof?**
No. The roof covering is not an eligible improvement. The grant can help pay for deck re-nailing, secondary water resistance and roof-to-wall connections, which are done during a replacement. You can estimate your total with our [roof cost calculator](/roof-cost-calculator).

**Can I start the work and apply afterward?**
No. Work started before written grant approval is disqualified from reimbursement.

**Is my house too new to qualify?**
If your home was built on or after January 1, 2008, it is not eligible for the grant.

**Do condos qualify?**
Not for this program. Condominium associations have a separate condo pilot program at [mysafeflcondo.com](https://mysafeflcondo.com).

**Is the program accepting applications right now?**
The program opens in phases, beginning with low-income homeowners 60 and older, and the schedule depends on funding. Check mysafeflhome.com for the current status.

**Who does the My Safe Florida Home inspection?**
A licensed inspector assigned by the program, not your contractor. The inspection report determines which improvements the grant can pay for.

**Does All Phase do My Safe Florida Home work?**

**What does All Phase do first on a grant project?**
A site visit and attic assessment, then a line-item proposal with a milestone payment schedule.

**Can one contractor do both the roof and the roof-to-wall strapping?**
We can. All Phase holds both a roofing license (CCC-1331464) and a general contractor license (CGC-1526236).

**What if I don't qualify for the grant?**
You can still get a free, no-obligation roof assessment from us, look at financing, and, if your home is eligible, get the program's free inspection to share with your insurance agent.
